Leaves don't have mouths or nostrils, but thousands of microscopic hydraulic gates open and close on their surfaces every day.

How stomata work:
1. Guard cells use water turgor pressure to bow outward and widen the stomatal pore.
2. Potassium ion transport drives osmosis, pulling water into guard cells when sunlight appears.
3. During dry spells, stress hormone ABA triggers rapid closure to stop dangerous moisture loss.

Did you know plants can control hydraulic valves without muscles or nerves?
