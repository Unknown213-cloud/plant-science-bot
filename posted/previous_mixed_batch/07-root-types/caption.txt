Beneath the soil, plants choose one of two distinct engineering strategies to survive.

Taproot vs Fibrous roots:
1. Taproots penetrate deep underground to access subterranean moisture and store rich reserves.
2. Fibrous root systems form dense horizontal mats that anchor plants and prevent topsoil erosion.
3. Delicate single-cell root hairs perform the heavy lifting of absorbing water and mineral ions.
