Water holds less than 1% of the oxygen found in air, yet fish swim effortlessly. How?

Inside fish gills:
1. Bony arches branch into feathery filaments lined with microscopic secondary lamellae.
2. An immense capillary network provides a vast, thin surface area for gas diffusion.
3. Countercurrent exchange flows blood opposite to water, capturing over 80% of available dissolved oxygen.

Did you know fish blood flows backwards relative to water to maximize oxygen?
