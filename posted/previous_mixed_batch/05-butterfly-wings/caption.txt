If you grind up a brilliant blue Morpho butterfly wing, the powder turns dull brown. Why?

The secret of structural color:
1. Butterfly wings are covered in thousands of overlapping microscopic scales made of chitin.
2. Vivid metallic blues are produced by structural nanostructures, not chemical pigments or dyes.
3. Microscopic ridges cause constructive light interference, reflecting only blue wavelengths.

Which is cooler: vibrant chemical pigments or nanostructure physics?
