An octopus has three beating hearts, blue blood, and stops its main heart when swimming.

How their circulatory system works:
1. The central systemic heart pumps blood to the mantle and arms, but pauses while swimming.
2. Two dedicated branchial hearts pump deoxygenated blood through the gills under pressure.
3. Their blood is naturally blue because copper-based hemocyanin carries oxygen in cold, dark water.
