A single leaf looks flat and simple, but under a microscope, it's a multi-story solar refinery.

Inside every leaf:
1. Palisade mesophyll cells stand upright in dense rows to absorb incoming light before photons escape.
2. Spongy mesophyll contains labyrinthine air spaces that let CO2 reach photosynthetic cells efficiently.
3. Vascular veins channel pressurized water via xylem and transport vital sugars through phloem.
